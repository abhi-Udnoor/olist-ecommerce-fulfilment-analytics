## 2. Business Justification

Reliable order delivery is an important part of the e-commerce customer experience.

Delivery delays can create customer dissatisfaction and may be associated with reduced subsequent purchasing behaviour.

Marketplace management therefore needs visibility into delivery-promise adherence, operational bottlenecks, geographical performance and customer experience.

This project demonstrates how historical marketplace data can be transformed into actionable operational and customer-experience insights.

## 3. Official SMART Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to evaluate historical logistics delivery-promise performance and investigate customer retention risk signals within an e-commerce marketplace using the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

By completion of the seven-phase project, the investigation will integrate and validate the available order, seller, customer, product, geographical and review information; establish measurable delivery-performance indicators; identify operational and geographical segments associated with delivery delays; and evaluate the relationship between poor delivery experiences, customer satisfaction and subsequent ordering behaviour.

The project will deliver a documented SQL Server analytical foundation, reproducible Python analysis, a validated KPI framework, an interactive Power BI executive dashboard and a final management report identifying at least three prioritised, evidence-supported operational improvement opportunities, where the available data supports them.

Customer retention findings will be interpreted using observed subsequent-order behaviour within an explicitly defined 90-day observation window. The analysis will not claim to establish contractual SLA breaches, actual customer churn or unsupported causal relationships.

**Completion milestone:** Final deliverables and project acceptance at the end of Phase 7.

## 5. Project Objectives

1. Establish the overall historical order and delivery-performance baseline.
2. Measure delivery-promise adherence and late-delivery severity.
3. Identify differences across operational, seller and geographical segments.
4. Investigate the relationship between delivery outcomes and customer ratings.
5. Compare 90-day subsequent-order behaviour across defined delivery-experience cohorts.
6. Produce an executive dashboard and evidence-based improvement recommendations.

**Excluded:** Contractual SLA verification, carrier-specific attribution, warehouse optimisation, actual logistics profitability and predictive churn modelling.
